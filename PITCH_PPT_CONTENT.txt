SLIDE 5 — THE MATH (Transparent, Not Black-Box)
================================================================================

  Heading: Transparent Valuation Formula

  V = Circle Rate × Area × MCR × f_loc × f_age × f_cfg × f_legal × f_floor

  Each factor:
    MCR      Market-to-Circle Ratio (1.05x–2.20x)  ← Location quality
    f_loc    Micro-location adjustment (±15%)       ← POI proximity
    f_age    Age depreciation (0%–39%)              ← Structure decay
    f_cfg    Config standardness (±15%)             ← Market fit
    f_legal  Legal clarity (0%–15% discount)        ← Title risk
    f_floor  Floor premium/penalty (±15%)           ← Accessibility

  "Every multiplier is bounded, tested, and auditable."

  47 unit tests verify the math to the rupee.

SLIDE 9 — FRAUD DETECTION (7-Layer Shield)
================================================================================

  Heading: 7-Layer Fraud Detection

  RULE-BASED:
  1. Size vs locality norms (z-score > 3σ = HIGH flag)
  2. Location-type mismatch (warehouse in residential zone)
  3. Config plausibility (4BHK in 400 sqft = impossible)
  4. Declared value outlier (> 2× market ceiling)
  5. Circle rate staleness (data > 3 years old)
  6. Rental yield anomaly (yield > 12% or < 0.5%)

  AI-POWERED:
  7. LLM anomaly detection (subtle cross-field patterns)

  DEMO: Our test correctly flagged "4BHK in 400 sqft, Pune" as
  config_area_mismatch [HIGH severity]
